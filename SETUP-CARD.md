# Bliss Sales Rep — Setup Card

Follow these steps in order. It should take about 5-10 minutes.

1. **Turn off training data sharing** in your Claude account settings, if you haven't already. (Ask your trainer if you're not sure where this is.)
2. **Open the Claude app** and select **Cowork** mode (next to Chat, above the message box).
3. Click **"Project or folder" → New project**. Name it **Bliss Sales Rep**. Leave "Use a folder" alone — you don't need one. Click **Create project**.
4. In the message box, paste exactly this and send it:

   ```
   Set yourself up using https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TrampettiMG/bliss-sales-rep/main/CLAUDE.md
   ```

5. Claude will look you up in QuickBase and show you your name, counties, and contact info. Check them and reply "yes" or fix anything that's wrong. You can also add the products you focus on, and — if you cover a lot of counties — the few you want lead searches to focus on. (If it can't reach QuickBase, it'll ask you a few quick questions instead.)
6. Claude will install all 12 tools and confirm setup is done. They show up in your project as: **Lead Finder, Research Brief, Call Prep, Email Writer, Content Builder, Bid Breakdown, Help Desk, Pipeline Check, New Leads, Forecast Helper, Update Logger, Quote Details.** You can ask for one by name ("run the Lead Finder") or just say what you want. Try typing: **"find leads for my county"** or **"summarize this bid"** (with a bid document uploaded). Other things you can try once you're set up: "research [a company/person/municipality]", "prep me for a call with [someone]", "draft an intro email to [someone]", "make me a one-pager for [HOAs/school districts/etc.]", or if you're not sure where to start, just ask "what should I use for..." or "how does this work."
7. From now on, just start new chats inside this same **Bliss Sales Rep** project — you never need to paste that link again. When your trainer says there's an update, type **"update my tools"**. To change your focus counties or contact info later, just say so ("change my focus counties to Henrico and Chesterfield").
8. If something doesn't work, stop and tell your trainer — don't try to fix it yourself.

That's it — you're set up.
